One line of text, at most 200 characters, that makes a stranger stop
scrolling and smile. Entry for the make.fun bounty
[One Line to Stop the Scroll](https://make.fun/bounty/u-febri-yudila-one-line-to-stop-the-scroll-e8b18425).

## The line

> My toddler handed the cashier a leaf at the market today and said, 'Keep the change.' The cashier gave him a grape. Fair trade.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`
(`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set):

```sh
node main.ts
```
